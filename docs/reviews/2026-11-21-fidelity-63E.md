# Fidelity review: 6.3E (`HutterPrizes/P63E.lean`), 2026-11-21

Fresh-context reviewer (Claude Opus subagent), read-only. **Verdict: faithful, with caveats.**

Claim we may use: "With ordinary conditional prefix complexity K(y|x) (the program gets the full condition x,
so |x| is free), there is no constant C, independent of n, k and ż, such that E_{k:n} ≤ C·2^{K(ż_{k:n}|ż_{<k})}.
This holds for AIξ with any fixed tie rule and for every other deterministic predictor (Lean-checked). The
mechanism is the length observation of Chernov-Hutter (ALT 2005, Lemma 5); we extend it from a one-step
deviation of M to error counts of every predictor."

1. "The expression" more naturally means the mass ratio ξ^SP(ż_<k)/ξ^SP(ż_1:n). The question is then a chain:
   (a) E ≲ ratio (= 6.3N, open) and (b) ratio ≲ 2^K (= 6.3R, fails; Lean-checked; essentially Chernov-Hutter
   Lemma 5). 6.3E refutes the end of the chain (no route gives E ≲ 2^{K(y|x)}), but does not decide 6.3N.
   Under the error-count reading, 6.3E answers the question directly.
2. Which K: ordinary prefix K with full condition is the literal reading; `exists_isOptimalPrefixConditional`
   makes the theorems non-vacuous. Problem 2.6 allows other K̃ variants; Chernov-Hutter's K_*(y|x*) (monotone
   in the condition) is not refuted by our witnesses (K_*(y|0^m*) ≈ K(m)); that version stays open.
3. Coverage: AIξ (6.15) with a fixed tie rule induces a predictor from the true prefix; `errors` = E_{k:n}
   with |x| = k−1. The result holds for every predictor, so it is a property of K(·|x), not of AIξ. C must be
   uniform in ż (else (6.17) gives the bound).
4. The book has "≈ 2^K" (600 dpi), not "≲": always say "within a multiplicative constant". With slack
   K(k−1) the bound becomes true (E+1 ≤ C·2^{K(k−1)+K(y|x)}; Pro 015 eq. 23, not Lean-checked). Lean statement
   uses d ≤ errors + 1 (weaker than the proof gives; correct). Lean proves unboundedness with K(y|x) ≤ b, not a
   rate. Novelty checked only against the two Chernov-Hutter versions.
