  independent of M; says nothing about (E) for fixed M.

(E) stays open for 𝒜, 𝓑 and the fair coin. A counterexample's quiet times (A_t/w_t → 0) form an immune set, and
each quiet run is at most C·log t long.

every late window of length O(log t). For uniformly nondegenerate µ, S_t ≥ c·2^{-K(t)} at the same rate. The
every-large-t version is open; the quiet times of any counterexample form an immune set."
