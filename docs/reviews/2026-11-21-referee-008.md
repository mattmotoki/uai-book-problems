## Novelty note (desk, 2026-11-21)

Hutter, "Optimality of universal Bayesian sequence prediction for general loss and alphabet", JMLR 4
(2003) 971-1000, arXiv cs/0311014, p. 988 (read from the PDF text): M = {μ₁, μ₂},
μ_{1/2}(1|x<t) = 1/2 ± ε_t, ε_t = min{1/2, √(ln w_{μ1}⁻¹ / (t ln t))}; for w_{μ1} ≪ w_{μ2},
E^{Θξ}_n − E^{Θμ1}_n ~ (1/ln n)·√(E^{Θμ}_n ln w⁻¹) → ∞. That is the regret of ONE mixture against the
informed predictor. It does not compare two mixtures ξ, ξ'. Same mechanism (bias → 1/2 at a slowly summable
rate), different quantity. Cite it as the antecedent. Finite regimes there: log/quadratic loss, and 0-1 loss
with margin |μ − 1/2| > ε.
