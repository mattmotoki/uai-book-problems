## Bauwens-Kuznetsov (arXiv 2610.18632v2, 19 Nov 2026) vs 023 (Claude Opus subagent, read in full)

M = any maximal lsc semimeasure (our 𝒜), conditional M_norm. Results: Thm 1.1 pointwise upper bounds on Σ e(n)^γ
over forced positions of a decidable tree (every branch); Thm 1.2 a lower bound on one constructed branch
(measure zero in every computable measure); Thm 1.3, Cor 1.4 online classification. No stochastic µ, no
expectation. Techniques: flow games (Andreev-Kumok style), forest partition. Shared tool: 2^{-K}-weighted
domination (standard). Overlap case (µ on a decidable tree, D = forced positions): their upper bound and our
Corollary 1 agree; neither implies the other.
